## Selected Work

Detailed case studies live on [tirthcshah.com](https://www.tirthcshah.com). Highlights:

### BlackTieCars
Co-founded and shipped a full-stack luxury transportation platform solo. Next.js, TypeScript, Stripe, Supabase, Google Maps. Generated $10K+ revenue and 3x business growth in 3 months through autonomous booking, payment processing, and AI-powered scheduling.

### TAMU Utilities Automation Suite
Architected 13 interconnected automation projects across 3 data sources, eliminating 95% of manual reporting work (10+ hours weekly). The 8-stage CHP Operations pipeline took 2.5 to 3 hour daily workflows down to 5 minutes (97% reduction), sustaining 99.5% automation success while processing 500,000+ daily sensor records.

### Equifax GCP Data Fabric
Migrated 7 fragmented external data aggregators into a unified GCP architecture with intelligent address standardization, deduplication, and match-key logic. Data hit ratio up 45%. Lookup turnaround dropped from 15+ minutes to under 1 minute.

### MAYS AI Chat Assistant
Built a production-grade chat assistant using Google Gemini 2.5 Flash with ChromaDB vector embeddings. Sub-3 second response on 80% of queries. 90%+ accuracy on business intelligence questions. Natural language to SQL generation lets non-technical stakeholders access $500K+ annual marketing analytics without data team support.

### TAMU Datathon 2025 (First Place)
Built a regulatory document classifier in a time-boxed competition.
